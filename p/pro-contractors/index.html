Our story

Started in college. Built on mentorship.

Founder and president James E. Jackson II started Pro-Contractors while studying at the University of Cincinnati, then put everything into it after graduating in 2003.

He credits much of that success to the goals he learned to set and reach from his own mentor: his father, James E. Jackson Sr.

Giving back

Pro-Con offers job opportunities and mentorship to city youth. Through the youth work program, an experienced Pro-Con technician helps prepare young people for the trade.
