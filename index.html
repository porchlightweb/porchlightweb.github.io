FAQ

 

Who owns the website?

You do. The site is yours, and I can hand over the files whenever you ask.

What about hosting?

Hosting is covered by the optional $39/month care plan, which also keeps your site online. If you'd rather host it elsewhere, I can give you the files.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. One round of changes is included with the website. After that, small edits are covered by the care plan, or we can agree a price for bigger changes.

Do I pay before seeing anything?

No. You see a free preview first and only pay if you want to go ahead.

Is there really an AI involved?

Yes. Jarvis, my AI assistant, helps me draft and build. I review the work and I'm the person you talk to.
