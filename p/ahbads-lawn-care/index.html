More than mowing

Ahbad's Lawn Care Service was founded by owner Hassan Embry on dedication, commitment, discipline and hard work. Part of the mission is mentoring young people in Cincinnati, mostly in minority neighborhoods.

Planting workshops & community gardens

Each year the team hosts planting and yard workshops, where people learn basic planting and get hands-on yard care experience, and helps start small community gardens that grow food for the participants.

Every contractor is trained to the same standard of customer service.
