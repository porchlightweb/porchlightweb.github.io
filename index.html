Why a website matters

Most customers look you up before they call.

If they can't find you, or what they find looks out of date, they often call someone else. A simple website fixes that.

People search first

When someone needs a plumber or a haircut, they search on Google. A website helps you show up with your hours, services and phone number right there.

It works 24/7

Your website answers the basic questions while you're on a job, closed for the night, or on your day off.

It looks professional

A proper website looks more established than a Facebook page alone, and it's yours: no algorithm deciding who sees it.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can't find your answer? Just email me.

Who owns the website?

You do. The site is yours, and I can hand over the files whenever you ask.

What about hosting?

Your site can be hosted for free on GitHub Pages, or hosting is included in the optional $39/month care plan, which also keeps your site online. If you'd rather host it somewhere else, I can give you the files.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. One round of changes is included with the website. After that, small edits are covered by the care plan, or I'll quote you a price for the change before doing it.

Do I pay before seeing anything?

No. Nothing is paid before the preview. You see a free preview of your site first and only pay if you want to go ahead.

Is there really an AI involved?

Yes. Jarvis, my AI assistant, helps me draft and build the site. I'm the person you talk to, and I'm your contact the whole way through.
