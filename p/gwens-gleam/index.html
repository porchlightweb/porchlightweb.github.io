The seven-step clean

The same checklist on every job, office or home.

  1. Proper clothing, protective gear and equipment
  2. High dust, then low dust
  3. Strip the area
  4. Change can liners
  5. Virex and Clorox clean
  6. Vacuum, sweep and mop
  7. Inspect the work
