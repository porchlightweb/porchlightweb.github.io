1. Who we are

Porchlight Web is run by Noah. An AI assistant, Jarvis, helps build sites and answer emails, but Noah checks the work and is responsible for it.

Contact: cozy-season@inkboxmail.com

2. The free preview

The preview is free and there's no obligation. If you don't like it, you don't owe anything.

3. The website package

The website is $499, one-time. It includes:

  • Up to 5 pages
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • Tap-to-call and email buttons
  • A Google Maps embed
  • 1 round of changes after delivery

4. Changes

A "round of changes" means one list of edits sent together: things like text, photos, colours and layout tweaks. It doesn't cover a full redesign or new pages.

Extra changes or extra pages are quoted before any work starts, so you always know the price first.

5. Payment & timing

  • You pay by a secure invoice link (Shopify checkout). Porchlight Web never sees your card details.
  • Work on the full site starts after payment.
  • The target is delivery about a week after payment and after receiving any content needed from you (like photos or wording).

6. Refunds

If your site hasn't been delivered within 30 days of payment for reasons on Porchlight Web's side, you can ask for a full refund. Once the site has been delivered, payment is non-refundable.

7. Who owns the site

Once you've paid, you own your site's content and files, and you can take them anywhere.

Porchlight Web may keep a small "Site by Porchlight Web" credit in the footer. If you'd rather not have it, just ask and it'll be removed.

8. Your content

When you send text, logos or photos, you're confirming you have the right to use them. Porchlight Web only uses real information that you provide or that's publicly available about your business.

9. Hosting & domains

Your finished site is published on a free hosting account in your own name (for example GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages), which Porchlight Web helps you set up. Before launch, a review copy may be shown on porchlightweb.github.io. If you'd like your own domain name (usually about $10–15 a year from any domain registrar, paid by you), Porchlight Web will connect it for you.

10. Care plan (optional)

  • $39/month for small edits, a monthly check for broken links and outdated info, and help with your domain. Hosting itself is free on your own account and isn't part of the care plan.
  • Billed monthly by invoice.
  • Cancel any time by email. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you've paid for.
  • No long-term contract.

11. Keeping sites online

Porchlight Web makes a reasonable effort to keep sites online. GitHub Pages and other third-party services are outside our control, though, so uptime can't be guaranteed.

12. Liability

Porchlight Web's total liability to you is limited to the amount you've paid.

13. Privacy

  • This site uses no tracking cookies and no analytics.
  • The contact form simply opens your own email app with your details filled in. Nothing is stored on this site.
  • Emails you send are used only to reply to you and to do the work. They're never sold.

14. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
